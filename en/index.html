Teddy – Pet Care Diary

In short

  • Teddy is home for everything about your pets: for dogs, cats, rabbits and the whole pack.
  • Your pet lives in the app. It’s happy when you pet it, gets sleepy when it’s late, and reminds you when something is due.
  • Free, no ads, no tracking. In English and German. For iPhone, coming soon to the App Store.

Everything at a glance

  • Profile with microchip and passport number, breed, birthday and emergency card
  • Vaccinations, deworming, medication and weight, with reminders before anything is due
  • Heat-cycle calendar with forecast for female dogs
  • Vet report as a PDF: reason for the visit, current condition and the last few weeks at a glance
  • Simply scan documents like the pet passport

A day with your pet

  • Daily routine with meals, walks, play and grooming to tick off
  • Paw Mail: Teddy tells you what’s on today
  • Appointments land in your calendar automatically if you like
  • Feeding, petting and cuddling, with real animal sounds
  • Home Screen widget

Caring together

  • Pack: family and partners look after the same pets
  • Temporary pet sitting: share via link or QR code with your pet sitter, including the daily routine and notes

Your data stays yours

Teddy has no server of its own. Everything stays on your device and, if you like, in your own iCloud. More in the privacy policy.
