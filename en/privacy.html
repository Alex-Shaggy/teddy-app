Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 1, 2026

In short

  • Teddy has no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools, and no built-in third-party services.
  • There is no Teddy server. Your pets' data lives on your device and, if you leave it switched on, in your own iCloud.
  • The provider of Teddy cannot see your pets, photos, documents or appointments.
  • The provider only receives what you explicitly send via “Feedback & Wishes”.
  • You can delete everything in the app at any time: Settings › your profile picture on the dog tag › Delete account.

2. Data on your device

Teddy stores everything you enter in a database on your iPhone or iPad. This includes:

  • your pets' profiles (name, species, breed, birthday, microchip and passport number, figure or photo)
  • your pets' health data: vaccinations, medication, weight, heat cycles, vet reports
  • appointments, reminders, daily routines, care logs and notes for sitters
  • photos, scanned documents (e.g. the pet passport) and voice recordings
  • contacts you create (e.g. vet practice, dog groomer)
  • insurance policies and loyalty cards (e.g. from the pet shop) with their numbers
  • your own details on the “dog tag” (name, phone, email, address) and your profile picture
  • cuddle and bond data as well as your settings

This data only leaves your device through the features described in the following sections. The Teddy widget on your Home Screen receives a small copy (pet names, photos, mood and cuddle counts) via a protected app group, which also stays on your device only.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, i.e. providing the app features you use. Access to your device's storage is strictly necessary for this (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

Health information about animals is not health data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. Teddy nevertheless treats it with the same care.

3. iCloud sync

If the “iCloud sync” switch under “iCloud & Backup” is turned on (default), Teddy syncs your data via CloudKit. CloudKit is a service provided by Apple. The data is stored in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account, so you have it on all your devices.

  • The provider of Teddy has no access to this private database.
  • The processing is carried out by Apple under your iCloud agreement. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply (apple.com/legal/privacy).
  • If you have turned on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud in iOS, Apple additionally encrypts the data end to end.
  • You can turn off syncing at any time. Everything then stays on this device only.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4. Sharing your pack and temporary pet sitting

Pack: If you invite other people to your pack, Teddy shares your pets with their Apple Account via an iCloud share (CloudKit Sharing). Invited people can then see and edit your pets' data, including photos, documents, health data and contacts. Completed tasks are shown with the name of the person who completed them. In doing so, Apple passes the names and Apple Accounts of the participants on to the other pack members.

Temporary pet sitting: For a pet-sitting arrangement, Teddy creates a share link or QR code. Anyone who has this link and is signed in with an Apple Account can open and edit the pets in this arrangement until you end it. So only pass the link on to people you trust with your pet. After the pet sitting has ended, the sitter keeps a read-only memory of the pets on their device.

You decide whom you invite. You can end shares at any time under “Pack & Pet Sitting”. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. If you enter other people's data (e.g. a sitter's), please make sure they agree.

5. Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. You can also use Teddy as a guest. If you sign in with “Sign in with Apple”, Apple passes on to the app:

  • an anonymous user identifier that is only valid for Teddy
  • if you choose, your name and email address (or a relay address generated by Apple)

This information is stored only on your device, the identifier in the protected iOS keychain. Teddy uses it to address you and to prefill your dog tag. It is not sent to the provider, as Teddy has no server of its own. You can unlink your Apple Account in the iOS Settings under Apple Account › Sign in with Apple.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

6. Feedback & Wishes

If you send a message via “Feedback & Wishes”, the following data is transmitted:

  • the type of message (feedback or feature request) and your text
  • the app version as well as the device type and iOS version (e.g. “iPhone · iOS 27.0”)
  • technical CloudKit details: the time and a pseudonymous CloudKit identifier of the sender (not your name and not your email address)

The message is stored in Teddy's public CloudKit database, which Apple operates for the provider as a processor. Only the provider can read it. We use the messages solely to fix bugs and improve Teddy. We delete them as soon as they have been dealt with, at the latest after 24 months.

Because Teddy does not send any contact details, we cannot reply directly. Please don't include sensitive information in the message. If you would like an answer, write to us at the email address in section 1.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is fixing bugs and improving the app. Sending a message is voluntary.

7. Notifications

Teddy schedules reminders, greetings from your pets and notes for sitters locally on your device. To receive changes from iCloud and shared packs, CloudKit uses the Apple Push Notification service, for which Apple manages a technical device token. No content about your pets is sent to the provider in the process. You can turn off notifications at any time in the iOS Settings.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your permission in iOS) and (b) GDPR.

8. Calendar, camera, microphone and photos

Teddy only asks for a permission when you use the corresponding feature. You can revoke any permission in the iOS Settings.

  • Calendar: Teddy adds your pets' appointments to the iOS calendar and only updates or removes the appointments Teddy created itself. If your calendar is connected to an account (e.g. iCloud, Google, Exchange), that account's provider syncs the appointments.
  • Camera: for pet photos, scanning documents, reading a pet-sitting QR code and the barcode of a loyalty card. The analysis happens on the device.
  • Microphone: for voice recordings of your pet. Recordings are only saved if you keep them.
  • Contacts: via the iOS contact picker, Teddy only receives the contact you select – not your entire address book.
  • Photos: via the iOS photo picker, Teddy only receives the pictures you select. If you wish, Teddy saves slideshow videos to your library without reading the library.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

9. Vet report, emergency card and sharing

Teddy creates vet reports, emergency cards and slideshows on your device. If you send them by email or via the share menu, you decide on the recipient and the channel. The transfer then takes place via the app you choose (e.g. Mail) and its provider.

10. Backups

Teddy creates a daily backup as a file in the app folder on your device. Via “iCloud & Backup” you can also export backups. The app folder is visible in the Files app and may be part of your iCloud or computer backup. The backups contain all of your pets' data, so only pass exported files on to people you trust.

11. Maps and websites

If you tap an address, Teddy opens Apple Maps, which sends the address to Apple. If you tap a website (e.g. that of your vet practice), your browser opens, and the privacy policy of that website applies.

12. App Store and crash reports

When you download and update the app, Apple processes data about your purchase or download. The provider only receives aggregated, anonymous statistics. The provider only receives crash reports and usage statistics if you have agreed to this in iOS (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › “Share With App Developers”). These reports do not contain any of your pets' data.

13. Recipients and transfers to third countries

Teddy only uses services provided by Apple (iCloud/CloudKit, Sign in with Apple, push notifications, App Store). For users in the EU, the responsible entity is Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Cork, Ireland. Data may be transferred to Apple Inc. in the USA. Apple bases such transfers on the EU Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or on EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Beyond that, the provider does not pass on or sell any data.

14. Retention period

  • Data in the app: until you delete it, delete your account or remove the app
  • iCloud: until you delete the data in the app or in the iOS Settings (Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage › Teddy)
  • Feedback: until it has been dealt with, at the latest 24 months

Delete account (Settings › your profile picture on the dog tag) removes all pets, appointments, photos, documents, backups and settings from this device and from your iCloud, and ends shared packs.

15. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

You manage most of the data yourself directly in the app: you can view, change, export it as a file and delete it. For everything else, an email to the address in section 1 is enough.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

16. Automated decisions

There is no automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Teddy calculates the pet figures' moods, reminders and heat-cycle forecasts exclusively on your device.

17. Children

Teddy is aimed at pet owners. Children under 16 should only send feedback with their parents' consent and only use pack or pet-sitting shares together with them.

18. Security

The data is stored in the protected app environment of iOS. It is encrypted by the device lock and is only transferred to iCloud over encrypted connections. Protect your device with a passcode so that no unauthorized person can access your pets' data.

19. Changes

As Teddy evolves, we will update this privacy policy. You can always find the current version in the app under Settings › Privacy and on the Teddy website.
